Our story

More than just coffee

Bean & Brew started as a tiny roastery in 2018. We wanted to create a space where neighbours could slow down, share stories, and enjoy honest food.

Today we still roast our beans in small batches, bake our pastries before sunrise, and greet you by name. Come for the coffee, stay for the warmth.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer dairy-free milk alternatives?

Yes — oat, almond, soy, and coconut milk are available for all drinks at no extra charge.

Can I order online for pickup?

Absolutely. Place your order through our website and pick it up at the counter — we’ll have it ready.

Are your pastries baked in-house?

Every single one. Our baker Leo starts at 4am so you get warm croissants and muffins fresh each morning.

Do you take table reservations?

Yes, for groups of 2–8. Use the reservation form below or give us a call.
